# 02 Product and scope

## Positioning
A calm, tactile, single-mechanic puzzle. The feeling to aim for: sliding a thread out of a woven cloth. Quiet, paper-like, unhurried. No timers, no pressure, no noise.

## Core loop
Home -> Level select -> Play -> Complete sheet -> next level. Average level length: 20 s to 4 min depending on chapter.

## Meta systems (all local)
- Progression: levels unlock sequentially. Chapters group 20 levels each and have a name and a one-line description.
- Stars: 3 stars for zero mistakes, 2 for one or two, 1 otherwise. Best stars stored per level.
- Mistakes: each level allows 3 mistakes (shown as three small painted dots under the board). Tutorial levels 1-5 allow unlimited mistakes. On the third mistake the Failed sheet appears with Retry.
- Hints: each level has 3 hint uses from a global hint bank. Bank starts at 5, gains 1 for every 3 levels completed, capped at 9. A hint gently outlines one currently removable thread in the accent colour for 2 s. If the bank is empty the Hint button is visibly disabled and says so in its label. No purchases, no ads.
- Undo: not offered. Exits are not reversible by design.
- Restart level: always available in the pause sheet.
- Stats: levels cleared, total stars, total threads cleared, perfect levels, longest perfect streak.

## Settings
Theme (5 options plus Follow system for light/dark pairing), Sound, Haptics, Reduce motion (overrides system default), How to play, About (version, licences via showLicensePage, privacy statement saying nothing is collected), Reset progress (confirm sheet).

## First-run experience
Splash (under 700 ms, no fake loading) -> Home. Level 1 opens with a quiet coach mark: one sentence, a pulsing outline on the free thread. No multi-page tutorial. Levels 1-5 teach: tap a free thread, blocked threads bounce, order matters, longer paths, dead-end pockets.

## Copy tone
Short, human, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no jokes at the player's expense. Examples: "Level complete", "Three clean pulls", "That one is blocked", "Nothing left to unwind".

## Non-goals
Ads, IAP, accounts, cloud sync, social, level editor, iOS polish.
